Questions About a Free Estimate

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The on-site walkthrough and the written scope are free. You are not charged to have us measure the yard or explain the base and drainage.

How soon can you come out?

Call 818-297-2475 and we will find the next open walkthrough. Some Google reviews mention a wait when the crew is booked. We tell you that date up front.

Do you give prices over the phone?

Not a final number. Price depends on demolition, grade, turf pile, and drainage. We measure on site and send a written number tied to a specific spec.

What should I have ready?

Your address, whether it is the front or back yard, a rough square footage if you have it, and whether you have dogs. Photos help if you cannot meet in person at first.

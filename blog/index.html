What a Cheap Turf Bid Usually Leaves Out

The number on a turf bid is not the pile height. It is the base. If the estimate does not name demolition, compaction, drainage, and edge restraint as separate lines, you are comparing a lawn to a rug. That is why Encino bids for the same yard can run from ten thousand to fifty-five thousand.
